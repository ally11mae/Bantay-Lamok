The Problem

A trap that isn't working can become a breeding site.

Ovitraps only work while they hold water and stay undisturbed. A dried-out or knocked-over trap stops doing its job. Most people find out only when they remember to check.

Bantay-Lamok takes over that checking. It doesn't count mosquitoes or detect disease. It simply tells you when your trap needs attention.

Normal

Water and conditions are in range. No text sent.

Needs Service

Low water or the trap was disturbed. You get an SMS.

Sensor Error

A sensor keeps failing. You're told to check the unit.

Node Offline

The hub spots a trap that has stopped reporting.

Why Bantay-Lamok

Built for real homes, schools and communities

Simple hardware, honest alerts, and a design that works where the internet doesn't.

Text alerts, not apps

A 4G LTE module sends SMS straight to your phone. No home Wi-Fi, no cloud account, no subscription to our service.

One hub, many traps

Trap nodes talk to a single hub over ESP-NOW. One SIM card covers them all, which keeps cost per trap low.

Four sensors, fewer false alarms

Water level, lid disturbance, temperature and humidity, and battery are checked together before any alert goes out.

Quick Look

How it works in 3 steps

Sensors watch the trap

A small node on your ovitrap checks water, lid activity, temperature, humidity and its own battery.

The hub decides

The hub combines every reading and classifies the trap as Normal, Needs Service or Sensor Error.

You get a text

Only when action is needed. Reply to acknowledge, and get a weekly summary even when all is well.
