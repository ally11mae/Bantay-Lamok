Before you buy

Is there a subscription?

No subscription from us. There is no cloud service to pay for. You only pay for the SIM load you use for SMS.

Can I add more nodes later?

Yes. One hub serves several nodes, which is why cost per trap goes down as you add more. Extra nodes cost ₱1,200 (DIY) or ₱1,500 (Ready) each.

Do you offer bulk orders for schools or barangays?

Yes, get in touch through the contact page and we'll discuss quantities and setup.

When will kits ship?

Kits are currently in pre-order while we complete field testing, so timelines will be confirmed after you reach out.
