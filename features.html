Features

Everything your trap needs to ask for help

Low-cost sensors, a smart hub and plain SMS, built to work where connectivity is limited.

Water-level sensing

Knows when the trap is running low or has dried out, the most common reason an ovitrap stops working.

Lid & disturbance detection

An IR break-beam sensor flags when the lid is opened or the trap is moved. Experimental feature, being validated.

Temperature & humidity

A DHT22 sensor adds environmental context to every reading so the hub can make a better decision.

Sensor fusion

Readings are cross-checked before classification, which cuts false alerts compared with single-sensor monitoring.

SMS over 4G LTE Cat-1

Alerts go to your phone through the cellular network. No home Wi-Fi, no internet and no cloud service required.

Battery-smart nodes

Nodes wake, sense, send and sleep on a cycle to extend battery life, and report their own battery voltage.

Hub-and-node design

Nodes link to the hub over ESP-NOW. One hub and one SIM serve several traps, so cost per trap drops as you add more.

Reply to acknowledge

Reply to an alert by text so everyone knows it's being handled. Rate limiting prevents repeat spam.

Weekly summary

A short text each week confirms that everything is running, even when no alert was needed.

Smart Alerts

Texts that tell you what and where

Each alert names the trap, its location, the reason and the latest readings, so you know exactly what to do.

Works With

One monitor, many trap types

Bantay-Lamok watches the condition of the trap, so it isn't tied to one design.

DOST OL Trap

The original deployment: the standard black container used in public school programs.

DIY bucket trap

Home-made bucket and cup ovitraps for the yard or garden.

Plant-pot trap

Pot-style traps for gardens and nurseries.

What it does not do. Bantay-Lamok monitors trap condition only. It does not count eggs or larvae, identify mosquitoes, or detect dengue or any other disease. It is not a medical device.
