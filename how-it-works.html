How It Works

From sensor to your phone

A simple chain: sense, decide, alert. Here's what happens inside, and how to set it up.

The system at a glance

Every kit has one hub and one or more trap nodes.

🪣

Hub

1. Sense

Each node wakes on a schedule, reads its sensors, sends a packet to the hub and goes back to sleep to save battery. Packets carry a sequence number so the hub can tell a lost message from a quiet sensor.

2. Decide

The hub checks that readings are valid, fuses them, and classifies the trap as Normal, Needs Service or Sensor Error. Bad readings are discarded and re-read instead of triggering false alarms.

3. Alert

If action is needed, the hub texts the saved numbers through its 4G LTE module. Normal readings are only logged, so you aren't spammed.

Getting Started

Set up in 5 steps

1

Unbox and prepare the SIM

Insert a 4G-compatible SIM into the hub and make sure it has load for texting.

  • Check there's cellular signal where the hub will sit
  • Charge the batteries using the built-in USB charger
2

Mount the node on your trap

Attach the node's bracket to your ovitrap and place the water-level probe in the water. Align the IR beam across the lid opening.

3

Place the hub

Put the hub within wireless range of your nodes, sheltered from rain and direct sun. Switch it on.

4

Register your phone numbers

Open the hub's local dashboard (see below) and add the numbers that should receive alerts, then set the trap names and locations.

5

Test it

Lift the water probe out of the water. Within one reporting cycle you should receive a "Needs service" text. Reply OK to acknowledge.

Local Dashboard

How to open your dashboard

The hub makes its own Wi-Fi network, so you can see every trap's status without any internet.

  1. Power on the hub and wait about a minute for it to start.
  2. On your phone or laptop, open Wi-Fi settings and join the hub's network, named Bantay-Lamok or as printed on your kit.
  3. Open a browser and go to the address shown in your setup guide, for example 192.168.4.1.
  4. Sign in with the credentials from the kit's QR-code setup card.
  5. Browse trap status, history, alert log and settings.

Good to Know

Questions

Do I need Wi-Fi or internet at home?

No. Alerts go out by SMS over 4G LTE. The dashboard runs on the hub's own Wi-Fi and doesn't need internet either. You only need a SIM card with load.

Does it detect mosquitoes or dengue?

No. Bantay-Lamok monitors the condition of the trap (water, disturbance, environment and battery) and reminds you when it needs attention. It isn't a medical device and makes no disease claims.

What happens if there's no cellular signal?

SMS delivery depends on network coverage at the hub's location. Check signal before installing, and place the hub where reception is good.

How often does it report?

Nodes report on a schedule, for example every few minutes in the prototype. The hub texts you only when action is needed, plus a weekly summary.

Does it work with my trap?

It's designed for the DOST OL Trap, DIY bucket traps and plant-pot traps. Mounting brackets are provided in the Ready Kit.

How long does the battery last?

Battery life is being measured in testing. Nodes use a wake-sense-send-sleep cycle to extend it, and the hub warns you when a node's battery is low.
